Project

Protective & Marine

Connecting facilities to coating-system recommendations

An interactive product-selection experience combining structured UI with 3D facility visualization to help users understand where protective coatings are used and find relevant system recommendations.

Challenge

Making a technical decision easier to navigate

Selecting a protective coating depends on where it will be used, the equipment involved, the substrate, and environmental conditions. The challenge was to turn those variables into a clear visual journey without losing the technical depth users need to make a decision.

Idea / approach

Start with the environment, not the catalog

Instead of beginning with a list of products, the experience begins with the facility. Users move from facility → area → equipment → substrate → recommended system, maintaining the real-world context behind each decision.

A direct, non-3D path provides an alternative for users who prefer a more traditional selection process.

My role

Bringing the experience to life

I led the visual design and prototyping in collaboration with UX, product, and development. My work included high-fidelity UI, interactive prototypes, integrating the 3D environment with the selection experience, and developing facility visualization and camera content.

The experience

From facility to system recommendation

The experience progressively narrows a complex technical decision into a series of understandable choices, moving from the physical environment to detailed coating-system information.

Building the environment

Building a facility that could also function as an interface

The environment needed to do more than look realistic. Each treatment area had to remain recognizable and provide enough context for users to understand where coatings would be applied. I combined existing and newly created 3D assets, established materials and vegetation, composed the scenes, and created camera movements used throughout the experience.

Motion

Using motion to create orientation and scale

Camera movements connect the overall facility with individual treatment areas, helping users understand scale and spatial relationships while moving between the environment and the product-selection experience.

Result

Connecting the physical environment to the product decision

Camera movements connect the overall facility with individual treatment areas, helping users understand scale and spatial relationships within the environment.

Final closing

The goal wasn’t to simplify the technical information. It was to make the path through that complexity easier to understand.
